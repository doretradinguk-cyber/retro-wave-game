## Commercial-rights rule

Free download does not automatically mean commercial permission.

For every external asset record:
asset_name
source
creator
licence
commercial_use
modification_allowed
attribution_required
download_date
source_url
notes

Do not mark commercial use as approved unless the source/licence supports it.

## Asset manifest example

## Visual filter

Every item should pass this question:

Does this help produce the Neon Hollow B / First Person look?

Target style:
- Cyan / teal
- Hot magenta
- Deep blue / black
- Neon emissive lighting
- Wet reflective floors
- Decayed luxury
- Illustrated / comic influence

Do not fill the repository with unrelated retro assets.

## Important rights note

AI asset services, texture libraries and similar tools may apply different licences to different tiers. Verify the current licence of the exact tier/source before an item becomes shipping material.
